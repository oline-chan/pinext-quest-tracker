Privacy Policy — Quest Tracker

Last updated: 20 November 2026. This is a hackathon demo policy for Pi Next. It must be reviewed by the app owner before a public launch.

What we collect

  • Pi identity: when you sign in with Pi Authentication, we receive your Pi user ID and username from the Pi SDK. We use them only to show your profile and save your quest progress.
  • Quest progress: which quests and steps you complete, your XP, streaks, and check-in dates.
  • Payment records: Pi payment IDs, amounts, memos, and status (approved / completed), as required to unlock the Premium Quest Pack. We never see or store your Pi Wallet passphrase or private key.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect passwords, wallet passphrases, private keys, or payment credentials. Pi payments are processed by Pi Network; your Pi Wallet passphrase never leaves the Pi Browser / Pi Wallet.

How data is used

Your data is used only to run Quest Tracker: showing your progress, the leaderboard, and unlocking content you paid for. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data retention and deletion

Quest progress is stored on the app server. You can ask for your data to be deleted by contacting the app owner through the Pi Next submission contact details. In the demo, data lives in a local JSON file (data/db.json) and is deleted when that file is removed.

Third parties

Pi Authentication and Pi payments are provided by Pi Network (Pi SDK, Pi Platform API). Their handling of your Pi account data is governed by Pi Network's own terms and privacy policy.

Contact

Quest Tracker is an independent Pi Next hackathon entry. Contact details will be added by the app owner at submission time.
